## 二、★★★ 「超导」的出处找到了 —— Vernon Roth（TeslaTech 2006）

> **全文**：87,490 字符（本库已收录）

### 2.1 ★★★ 核心段落（"高自旋超导水"）

> 「[水从顶部流入，在这里作环形涡旋]… This literally **structures water**, and you capture
> a certain amount of a certain type of the water down here…
> **All of the water in there is captured as a high-spin superconducting water.**
> Now, **we know that it's superconducting** because one of the very interesting side effects was
> we took this thing apart and started using it to evaporate salt out of salt water…
> And what we found very interestingly was that **salt was growing out of the side of the
> stainless steel container**. Literally, salt crystals were not climbing up and evaporating over here,
> they were **growing out of the side of the stainless steel**.
> **The steel had become superconductive, and salt was traveling through it.**
> …So, we've got water that is no longer behaving like water. **It's more like an oil.**」

| # | 主张 | 属性 |
|---|---|---|
| ① | 涡旋处理后的水 = 「**高自旋超导水**」（high-spin superconducting water） | ⭐ 圈内主张 |
| ② | 「超导」的**判据**：把装置拆开用于盐蒸馏 ⇒ **盐晶体"从不锈钢侧面长出来"、盐"穿过"钢** ⇒ 认定**钢变成了超导** | ⭐ **实验主张**（★ 无独立复现） |
| ③ | 这种水「不再像水，更像油」 | ⭐ 圈内描述 |

### 2.2 「超导依赖于几何结构」

> 「magnetism, paramagnetism, conductivity, and… **conductivity and superconductivity
> all are contingent on the geometrical structure of the element** you're working with.
> …**A superconductor will conduct energy based on its crystal and structure.**
> A magnet [is a magnet] because of its crystal and structure…

⇒ ★ 「**几何结构决定超导**」——与库内 `21` 的"八面体磁场/相位共轭"（`25`）语境呼应。
