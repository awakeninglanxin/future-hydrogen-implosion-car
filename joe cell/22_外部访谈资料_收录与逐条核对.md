## 一、★★★ 三条主张找到原始出处（此前误标「无据」，已更正）

### 1.1 「死飞 / 20000 rpm」→ ★ 找到（**16,000 RPM**）

> **英文原文**（Charles Seiler 转述，位于文首 Joe 简介段）：
> "Joe has stated that he has witnessed engines running at **16,000 RPM**, with a Joe cell powering them, **without any gasoline at all**, and **the engine sounds very smooth at this RPM**."
>
> **中译**：Joe 说过，他见过发动机在 Joe cell 驱动下以 **16,000 转**运行、**完全不用汽油**，
> 而且在**这个转速下发动机声音非常平顺**。

🔵 **核对**：
- 老师口述「接近 **2 万转**」 ←→ 本文 **16,000 转**（**同量级，数字略低**）
- ★ 归属更正：这是 **Joe 本人的陈述**（经 Charles Seiler 转述），**不是"新增主张"**
- ⏳ **数字差异**（16,000 vs 20,000）待老师确认口径（是否指同一件事）

### 1.2 ★★★「越高越听不到」→ 找到，**而且是 Joe 亲口说的**

> **英文原文**（**访谈转录**，Joe 第一人称，对话方 BOB）：
> "We had the little escort, which is running on the videos, sitting where my scorpion is out here on the front lawn where you are now,
> **we had it idling at sixteen thousand revs with no sound**, no acceleration yet, and you could accelerate from there…"
>
> "He was sick, he walked up, he leant on the mudguard, and the car just went BUHHHHHHHHHHHH! And died out.
> I said get your hand off the mud guard, and the car just went straight back up to sixteen thousand revs."
>
> **BOB: SIXTEEN HUNDRED.**
> **Thousand!**
> **BOB: THOUSAND! IT WASN'T MAKING ANY NOISE.**
> **No, well you see we don't explode these motors.**
> **BOB: BUT YOU NORMALLY WOULD RUNNING ON PETROL**

🔵 **核对（★ 这条极其重要）**：
1. ★ **原文明确有两处"无声音"**：「**with no sound**」＋ 采访者复述「**IT WASN'T MAKING ANY NOISE**」
2. ★★ **而且 Joe 自己给出了原因**：**"we don't explode these motors"**（**我们不让这些发动机"爆炸"**）
   ⇒ ★★★ **这正是「内爆（implosion）而非爆炸（explosion）」主张的原始语境** ——
   本库在 `HHO书本精读` 里记录过同一逻辑：「**内爆不震耳，爆炸才震耳**」
3. ⏳ **数字注意**：访谈说的是「**idling at sixteen thousand revs**」（**怠速** 16,000 转）——
   与老师另一句「最低怠速 **60 rpm**」**不是同一个数字**（★ 待核）

### 1.3 「引擎声音」的机制说明（Joe 的解释，本库新收）

> "See, the **sound in the exhaust, is explosion**, and it thumps against our ear drums with pressure.
> And when we explode hydrogen and oxygen in the bubble, and **it doesn't hurt our ears**…
> Our ears, ear drums were made for thumping pressure against it.
> **The reason your ears hurt when you get an implosion is that your ears were…**"
>
> "…you can't hear anything, because it **distorts all of the sound** because of the explosions, and the pressure.
> But I can show you with these government agents going, seconds after we turned it from **petrol over to just the water cell**…
> they go to the exhaust pipe to smell what's coming out of the exhaust, and **it's a totally different thing** to p[etrol]…"

⇒ ★ **Joe 主张的因果链**：**不做"爆炸" ⇒ 排气声不同 ⇒ "听不到"**。
★ 这**修正**了本库 `19` 号的判定方向（见 §四）。

## 三、★ 附加收获（意外发现，价值很高）

### 3.1 ★★★ 「点火正时」——Joe 本人讲了具体角度

> **英文原文**（访谈转录，Joe 自述）：
> "…**advancing the timing on a cast-iron motor on eighteen to twenty degrees advance makes the motor run even smoother**, for a while. But you can't start them there."
>
> "…On a four cylinder turning the **distributor right around to quarter of the flaming turn**, maybe a bit more than a quarter.
> You work it out by **looking at the distributor**, and **the motor smoothes out and goes quiet**.
> And it's done **on video in front of you** with the people watching."

🔵 **核对 —— 与本库已有的「提前角数字谱」并列**：

| 来源 | 提前角 | 备注 |
|---|---|---|
| ★ **老师（2026-09-13）** | **8–12°**（泵齿轮错齿 ≈ 12°） | 现代柴油机 |
| ★★ **本访谈（Joe 自述）** | **18–20°**（铸铁机） | "makes the motor run even **smoother**" |
| ★★ **本访谈（Joe 自述，另一处）** | **分电器转近 1/4 圈**（≈ 90° 分电器角） | "the motor **smoothes out and goes quiet**" |
| 库内三源 `03 §A.4/B.4` | Joe **30°**（分电器）／Schiffer **35–40°**／Hilton **80°** | 见 `16` 号文档 |

⇒ ★ **观察**：这是一个**跨度极大的数字谱（8° → 90°）**，说明：
① 提前角是**逐车试出来的**（不是固定值）；
② ★★ **Joe 本人在两处都把它与「更平顺 / 安静」联系**（"smoother" / "goes quiet"）——
　这与老师说的「调正时」是**同一个动作**，但目的表述不同（老师侧重"平衡"，Joe 侧重"平顺/安静"）。

### 3.2 ★★ 「频率」的 14 处语境（含 Joe 的诚实自述）

> **★ 关键一处**（Joe 自述）：
> "I didn't change the cell, but **I found the frequency that was needed** without altering the cell. How's that?
> **BOB: HOW DID YOU FIND THAT FREQUENCY?**
> Ah, now, **I can't tell you. Because I really don't know, I just know.**
> **It's a knowing, it's not a doing, it's not a telling, it's not an explaining thing**, it is, that I know it."

⇒ ★ **诚实注记**：Joe **自己承认"频率"这件事他说不清**（"I can't tell you... I just know"）。
　对本库的「谐振」主张而言，这是一条**重要的边界说明**（★ **可复现性存疑**）。

其它语境速览：
- "the motor's **frequency**, the water cell, the water, the plates and everything else **lined up for it perfect**"（整体"对齐"）
- "if you take that cell and you put it into a car which is wired up through all these different relays and **frequency changes**… **That cell might not work**"（★ 频率环境依赖性）
- "the gauges were working off that **frequency of the car**"（仪表受频率影响）
- "you've now changed **four different voltages, four different frequencies, four different polarities**"

## 四、★ 对 `19` 号判定的修正（"听不到"这条）

| 项 | `19` 号原判定 | ★ 修正后 |
|---|---|---|
| **「20000 rpm 听不到 / 感受不到」** | ⚠️ **不符**（20000 rpm ⇒ 667 Hz 高音 + 69 吨力 ⇒ 只会更响更震） | ★ **分开两层看**：<br>· **燃烧层**：Joe 的**机制主张**是「**不做爆炸**（内爆）⇒ 排气声本就不同」——**这是他"听不到"的理由**（访谈原话：*we don't explode these motors*）<br>· **机械层**：**活塞/飞轮的应力只由转速决定**，与燃烧方式无关 ⇒ `_复算25` 的结论（MPS 90 m/s、飞轮轮缘 524 m/s）**不变**<br>⇒ ★ **判定改为**：「**机制层有其自洽解释（待验证）；机械层约束不变**」 |
| **数字** | 20000 rpm | ★ 原始出处写作 **16,000 rpm**（`19` 已标注两数并列） |

> ★ 这次修正的价值：本库此前把「听不到」当成"与物理直接矛盾"，
> **现在知道它有 Joe 自己的机制解释**（不爆炸 ⇒ 无爆震声）。
> ⇒ **判定从"不符"改为"机制可解释（待验证）＋ 机械约束不变"** —— 这更准确。
