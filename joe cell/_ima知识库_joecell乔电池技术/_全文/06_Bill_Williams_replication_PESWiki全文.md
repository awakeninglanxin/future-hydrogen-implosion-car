## 一、Introduction（原页正文）

**报道一 · PESN 2006-04-13**

> Joe Cell Truck Builder Threatened, Destroys Plans — After announcing that he had successfully built a truck that runs on Joe Cell technology, drawing energy from water and Orgone, Bill Williams said he was approached by two men who requested that he stop his research, threatening him with dire consequences if he didn't. Others are keeping it alive.

**★ 报道二 · PESN 2006-06-22（此前未取得）**

> Running a vehicle on Joe Cell is difficult and unstable — Bill Williams has said it took weeks to get his truck to the point of running 100% on the Joe cell, and that once achieved **the system was very unstable and dangerous**. Much R&D remains.

> 🔴 **这一段的份量，比"被威胁"那段更重**：
> **它是同一个信息源的后续报道，由同一当事人自己说"系统很不稳定、很危险"。**
> 圈内叙事通常只引 4 月那条（被威胁），**不引 6 月这条（他自己说不可靠）** —— 见 §五。

## 二、Discussion Group Posts（原帖摘录，共 3 条）

**① Ford Test Drive**（Yahoo Group `JoesCell2` message 16，2006-04-06）
Bill 描述他的 Ford 表现：

> "It the throttle response was very crisp or touchy. With about a **1/8" of movement** the next thing I new I was **booking at close to 80 mph**. If I lifted of ever so slightly on the throttle and it felt like I was putting the brakes on and the **speed would drop down to 30 mph or so**."

**② "Yes"**（message 33，2006-04-11）
Bill 对提问的回答：

> 问："have you been threatened by men touting guns" → 答：**"Yes"**

**③ Joe cell lid**（message 143，2006-04-17，署名 **Peter**）
对该车"运行不稳"的技术解释：

> The reason Bill's engine was erratic was because **the outside air was getting into the engine via breather holes, etc.** fuel pump, distributor, and PCV Oil filler cap. also tweaking the timing.

> 📌 **注意第 ③ 条**：**圈内自己给出的"不稳"原因是"漏气"（工程问题），不是"能量不够"。**
> 这与 §四 的官方后续报道口径**并列存在** —— 如实保留，不合并。

## 四、★ How he did it（John Carter，2006-04-19 记述）

> Bill Williams started building a **5 cylinder cell comprising 1", 2", 3", 4" and outer tube 5"**, but Peter later advised him to **remove the 1" centre tube** and go with only **two neutrals being the 3" and 4" tubes**.
>
> Peter says **it makes no difference whether the top is conical or flat** so Bill went with the easier option of a **flat lid**.
>
> **Bill had the connection to the vacuum line but the next step was to go blind** but as we all know he was advised not to continue.

### ★★ 与 `03_JoeCell装车核心技术要点` 的接口（本段直接印证）

| 本页记载 | 对应 `03` §五 的哪一步 |
|---|---|
| "**had the connection to the vacuum line**" | **阶段 A**（接进气道/真空管，通气的） |
| "**but the next step was to go blind**" | **阶段 B**（接盲塞）—— **他停在了 A，没进 B** |
| **"two neutrals being the 3" and 4" tubes"** | 中性管配置（**5 管 → 减为 3 管**，只留 3" 与 4" 作中性） |
| 外管 5"、内管 1"/2" | 与 `03` §五 的同心管口径表**可对照** |

> ★ **这段是 `03` §五「两阶段」的独立佐证**，而且它来自**2006 年当事人的一手技术记述**（经 John Carter 转述）。
> **它证实了"阶段 A → 阶段 B"这个划分不是编者后加的，圈内原始文档里就是这么描述的。**

## 五、★ 本页自己的"反向证据"（引者通常不引的部分）

> **原页在"Introduction"里同时放了 4 月与 6 月两条 PESN 报道。**
> **但圈内转述几乎只引 4 月那条（"被两个男人威胁"），6 月那条（"他自己说系统很不稳定、很危险"）极少被提。**

| 被广泛转述的 | 极少被转述的（同页并列存在） |
|---|---|
| 2006-04-11 两名男子登门、要求停止、销毁笔记 | 2006-06-22 **Bill 自称"要花几周才做到 100% 靠 Joe Cell 运行"、"一旦做到，系统非常不稳定且危险"** |

| 图注"Assembled cell, bubbling" | 图注**是猜的**（"from a guess"） |
| — | 原页自标"**pieced together from what others had**"（二手拼合） |
