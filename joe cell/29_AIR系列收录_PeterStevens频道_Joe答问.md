### 2.2 ★★★「内爆」是 Joe 自己的核心词（答问 61min · 7 处 implo）

**① 「cold implosion」（观众提问原词）**：

> "can you first describe in detail and in layman's terms … the wiring configuration
> that allows the engine to run on **cold implosion** …"

⇒ ★ 注意：**「冷内爆 / cold implosion」**——观众用它描述 Joe 的系统，Joe 没有纠正 ⇒ **这是圈内对该系统的正式称呼之一**。

**② ★★★ Joe 的活塞-内爆论述（答问 61min @15702–16566）**：

> "what happens is **there's an implosion occurs when you set off your spark inside the cell** …
> it's not an implosion such a vacuum — your piston is gone past top dead center down the power stroke…
> it actually goes to the top … then there's a **big expansion** … **the valves the pistons and everything else** …
> it's one of the **cold imploding engines**"

**③ ★★「活塞先用频率自然内爆→爆炸→另一端造真空→吸开阀」（同视频 @20343）**：

> "your piston is doing it **naturally imploding with the frequencies** first to get your piston to the top,
> it's on your **compression stroke**, and then **exploding and broiling down** … and then at the other end
> you haven't got enough so **she creates a vacuum** … and **sucks** the smallest thing opens…"

### 2.3 ★★★「喇叭/耳朵」段落 —— 内爆对耳膜的作用（又一次）

> "some of these bubbles … **some of these bubbles actually don't explode —
> they turn finally exploding into implosion** and **your ears** …
> **take pressure, that's what you realize you're doing** …
> you got **ringing in your head**, you got **hurting in your ears**…"

⇒ ★★★ **这是「内爆作用于耳朵」的又一处 Joe 原话**——而且说得更具体：**耳鸣（ringing）+ 耳痛（hurting in your ears）+ 压力**。
⇒ ★ 与 `23 §3.5`「内爆会把耳膜吸过去」、`26` 号（老师框架：**内爆也作用耳膜，只是"吸"而非"吹"**）**完全一致**。
⇒ 本库对「内爆不震耳」的**第二次证据加固**（第一次是老师指正）。

### 2.4 ★★★ Part 1 核心：spark 74 处的三重论述

**① 「spark doesn't ignite anything」（火花不点火）**：

> "when people try and figure out what is actually running the engine — **is it the spark? No,
> the spark doesn't fire** [the fuel air mix] … your spark is jumping from the center electrode
> over to ground. And **the spark isn't igniting anything. It's converting itself back into the block
> and back around through your battery in the whole circuit.**"

⇒ ★★ **机制主张**：火花的作用不是点燃混合气，而是**转回缸体 → 经电池构成回路**（与「特斯拉线圈 90° 尖峰」的"建场-塌缩"叙述同族）。

**② 「90° 尖峰」再次确认**：

> "the coil, **which is called a Tesla coil** in the cars … **It is a 90° spike.** …
> they create it, but it's **not them that it's actually been used up to make it** …
> your spark coming out of your coil is a **90° spike**. **You're not using** [positive or negative]"

**③ ★★★ waste spark management = 内爆（Joe 对「内爆」的工程解释！）**：

> "Commodore has used something called **waste spark management** … where they use
> **the positive side as well and fire it** … they **break the positive side** … the positive side
> is then fired into the **opposite cylinder** … **So, they actually created this implosion
> to fire on that side** … your V6 is really a **V12**, but firing on the compression and
> **firing on the exhaust**, and it makes a **smooth motor with good economy** …
> they're getting **38 to 40 miles per gallon** out of the V6s now because of waste spark
> （对比：原 V8 15–18 mpg；V6 无 waste spark 约 20 mpg）"

⇒ ★★★ **这段是本轮最重要的发现之一**：Joe 把「**内爆**」与**车厂真实工程（waste spark management）**连起来——
**在排气（非压缩）缸同步点火 = Joe 所谓"造出内爆"**；并给出**可核对的油耗数据（38–40 mpg）**。
⇒ ⚠️ 编辑者注：waste spark（无分电器双火花系统）是**真实的主流技术**（ Holden Commodore V6 确用）；但「排气缸点火 = 内爆」是 **Joe 的解释框架**——两侧缸一个在压缩末期点火、一个在排气冲程点火，物理上后者点火**无做功意义**（故称 "waste"）。**两轨并列记录**。

**④ Suzuki 极性反转完整版（读者来信 + Joe 回应）**：

> 读者来信：「I figured out what you did … with the Suzuki truck. **Joe, you have reversed
> the polarity of the entire system.** … When you **shut down the positive side of the coil,
> the alternator charges the system in the opposite direction** … The alternator is charging
> this way and your battery is charging the other way.」
> 另：Suzuki 化油器上有 **solenoid（电磁阀）**，用途是「**shut down the fuel from entering
> the intake manifold**」（切断进油）。

**⑤ DC 发电机线索**：

> "early vehicles had … **DC magneto**. And they **only had one wire** coming out of them.
> There's **no second wire**. And that one wire just went and screwed onto the engine …
> That was from a one cylinder up to a 12 cylinder motor."

## 三、★★★ ASTRO 演讲更新 —— 「60 rpm」最接近的实车数据

### 3.1 ★★★「低转速怠速」的完整机制论述（@18324）

> "There's **no perceivable compression** when you pull the string … **There is no compression**
> and therefore normally any internal combustion engine **trying to idle at low revs with 20 or 30°**
> the thing will just fire. **The piston will get stalled or go backwards. It won't run.**
> **With one of these you can go from top dead center back to 90° before top — halfway up stroke —
> and the engine just turns over.** ★★ **There's definitely an implosion component that happens
> before the explosion** present."

⇒ ★★★ 这段话把老师的框架与低转速现象**连起来了**：
| 要素 | 内容 |
|---|---|
| 「**没有压缩**」 | 手拉启动时**无可感知的压缩**（异常特征） |
| 「**正常机做不到**」 | 普通内燃机在低转速 + 20~30° 时**活塞会失速或反转** |
| ★★ 「**这套系统能做到**」 | **从 TDC 退回 90° BTDC（半程）仍能转动** |
| ★★★ 「**内爆先于爆炸**」 | 「**There's definitely an implosion component that happens before the explosion**」＝**老师框架的第三方独立表述** |

### 3.2 ★★★「200 RPM 城市行驶」实车数据（@44055）

> "It used to go into city finally at … **how many RPM? 200.** Yeah. Just way slight lower
> than it should have been **to the point the ignition light come on** …
> **I just could not stop this thing from dropping off into absolutely smooth. So smooth.
> Smoother than normal 800 RPM. Just down it goes.** And Bob's got his head up there
> and he's got his ear on the exhaust pipe, but **it's running as smooth as would be**."

| 数据 | 值 | 意义 |
|---|---|---|
| ★★ **城市行驶转速** | **200 RPM** | ★★★ **本库迄今最接近老师「60 rpm」的实车数据** |
| 对照 | 正常怠速 **800 RPM** | 200 = 正常的 **1/4** |
