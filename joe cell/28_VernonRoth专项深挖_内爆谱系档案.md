### 3.1 ★★★ 核心命题：内爆是「以太 → 能量」的机制

> 「what is the magnitude or what is the mechanism that allows ether to become energy —
> **and that mechanism is implosion.** … that is what allows us to take ether and condense it
> down into either energy, or physical reality, or all of the various incarnations between.」
>
> 「**etheric implosion is an internal repeating pattern — when it encounters itself,
> it generates an external field.** How it meets itself dictates what types of external fields it generates.」
>
> 「**etheric light implodes into a geometrical shape, creating a self-repeating fractal** —
