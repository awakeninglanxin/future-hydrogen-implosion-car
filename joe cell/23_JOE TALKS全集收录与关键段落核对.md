## 一、★★★ 最重要：「提前 8–12 度」的原始出处找到了（Tape 1，两处）

老师在 `16` 号文档追问过的「**点火必须提前 8–12 度**」——**这个数字在 Joe 语录中有两处原文**：

### 1.1 第一处：不同材质发动机的正时值（`@108389`）

> "Because did you know that **a cast iron motor in America, Canadian-American,
> you set them up to flaming twenty degrees advance.**
> **In Australian cast iron you set them down to somewhere around about eight to twelve.**
> There are different combinations in the cast iron.
> Now where do you set a cast-iron block with an aluminium head? **Three to eight.**
> Where do you set a complete aluminium motor? **Top dead centre.**"

中文：

> 「你知道吗——**美加产的铸铁机，正时设到 20 度提前**；
> **澳大利亚铸铁机，设在 8 到 12 度左右**；
> 铸铁缸体+铝缸盖的，**3 到 8 度**；
> 全铝机呢？**上止点**。」

紧接其后（同一节）：

> "But **advancing the timing on a cast-iron motor on eighteen to twenty degrees advance
> makes the motor run even smoother**, for a while. But you can't start them there."

### 1.2 第二处：活塞行程机制章（`@103873`）★ 与"内爆"直接相连

> "And then when we get to the top of the stroke, actually,
> **somewhere around eight to twelve degrees before the top of the stroke,
> we have spark which then explodes. But it doesn't explode. Or is it a kick backwards?**"

中文：

> 「到了行程上端——**大约上止点前 8 到 12 度**——我们打火，然后**它"爆炸"。
> 但它没有爆炸。还是说，是**反向踢**了一下？」」

### 1.3 与老师的呼应（★ 数字对上了）

| 来源 | 8–12 的用法 |
|---|---|
| **老师（2026-09-13）** | 「**点火必须提前 8-12 度**」（内爆变负压课题中提出） |
| **Joe 原话（Tape 1）** | ① **澳铸铁机标准正时 = 8–12 度**（与美加 20 度对照）② **活塞机制章：上止点前 8–12 度打火** |

⇒ ★★ **老师说出的数字与 Joe 语录完全一致**——不是自创，是**引用了 Joe 的原始设定值**。

## 二、★★★「排气口冰冷」的原话找到了（Tape 1 @98628）

> "…She just went WHHAAAHHHH! Straight back up to **sixteen thousand revs**.
> The tacho went straight over the needle and bent it, and the car just had a noise
> at first and then lost it. **You couldn't hardly hear the motor running at all.
> The exhaust was running with ice on it, the radiator had ice on it.
> You could put your hand on everything and it was freezing damn cold,
> and there was no noise in the motor because the damn thing wasn't exploding.**"

中文：

> 「……车直接轰的一声**冲到 16,000 转**，转速表的指针撞过头**撞弯了**；
> 起初还有点声音，随后就没了——**你几乎听不到发动机在转**。
> **排气管上结着冰，散热器上结着冰，你摸哪都是冰冷的；
> 发动机里没有噪音——因为它没有在"爆炸"。**」

★ 这一段同时覆盖了老师的**三个主张**：

| 老师说的 | 本段原文 |
|---|---|
| 「排气口是**冰冷的**」 | ✅ 「The **exhaust was running with ice on it**」 |
| 「速度越高**越听不到**引擎运转」 | ✅ 「**You couldn't hardly hear the motor running at all**」 |
| 「（内爆而非爆炸）」 | ✅ 「because the **damn thing wasn't exploding**」 |

> 📎 与 `20` 号 B 类的接口：此前「排气冷」的**最弱来源**是 `HHO书本精读/01`
> （书自标 `need verification`）；现在**有了一手原话**（Joe 亲述 + 冰的细节）。
> 但注意：**这是"主张的原话出处"，不是"物理上已证实"** —— 两件事分开记账。
>
> 📎 与 `19` 号「冷排气」复算的接口：卡诺两难（排气冷 vs 功率输出）的复算结论不变；
> 但**复算针对的是"理论不可能"，原话提供的是"当事人见证"** —— 分轨并列。

## 三、「内爆」语境全集（Tape 1，39 处命中 · 摘要）

这是「内爆负压」主张的**最原始一手语境**（1993 年 Joe 亲述）。核心段落：

### 3.1 软管「内爆测试」——一把钥匙（`@20288` 起）

> "…if you get an **implosion** in a cell … and you have a hose or anything else
> connected and you light it, then you get an implosion back into your container.
> You can hold it on your tongue … **there is no suction.**
> It's had an **implosion, not an explosion**, an implosion.
> **You put it on your tongue there is no sucking, no blowing. No pressure and no suction.**"

### 3.2 ★★★ 跟「charged water」才吸水（`@20587` 起）

> "…if you put that hose that had an implosion **into charged water**,
> it will go **kchherrrr!** It'll **suck water out of your vat, which is charged water**,
> into the container what imploded.
> **But you can put it into plain water, you can put it into air, you put it in anywhere else
> and you will not get a suction or a pressure, only when you put it into charged water** it will…"

中文：

> 「把内爆过的软管放进**带电水（charged water）**里，它会"咕咚"一声——
> **把槽里的带电水吸进那个内爆过的容器**。
> 但放进普通水、放进空气、放去任何别的地方，**你都得不到吸力或压力；
> 只有放进带电水，它才会吸。**」

★ **这是 Joe 体系里最"可检验"的机制描述之一**：内爆效应**只在"带电水"语境出现**
（与"处理过的水/活细胞"是同一主题簇）。

### 3.3 「车不是爆炸，是往回拉」

> "'…**the car isn't exploding**, and when you touch the accelerator,
> the motor goes WWRRRMM! WRRRMMM! **It pulls backwards.**'"

### 3.4 两者可同时存在

> "**But you can have implosion and explosion at the same damn time.**"
> （紧接着 BOB 追问：SO YOU RECKON THAT MOTOR WAS SPINNING AT SIXTEEN THOUSAND REVS?）

### 3.5 为什么"内爆"难听/伤耳朵（听觉机制，生动且可复述）

> "**The reason your ears hurt when you get an implosion is that your ears were never
> made for a suck, they were made for a blow.** And a big phenomenal blow they can handle,
> not much of a suck. So the implosion sucks your ear drums against it…"

> 📎 与库内 `HHO书本精读` 的「内爆不震耳、爆炸才震耳」是**同一逻辑**
> —— ★ 现在有了 Joe 原话版，两处可互证。

## 五、Tape 2/3/4 新内容摘录（Ritalie 版完全没有 · 约占全套 71%）

### 5.1 ★★★ 「正时大幅提前 → 一万转」的完整操作序列（Tape 2）

**第一处**（`@154545`，喷油嘴封堵演示的铺垫）：

> "…**we're gunna start that car**, but you won't be able to accelerate it if you touch
> the accelerator because there's no fuel… It's only on idle…"

**第二处**（`@155507`，核心）：

> "…but it's very dull. You can rev it, somewhere up to about five, six thousand revs.
> But it's very doughy, **I go around to the distributor and I advance the damn distributor
> up to somewhere around fifty degrees advance. And the motor starts to smooth out.**
> And now, you just touch the accelerator down to about a sixteenth to a quarter,
> not full acceleration, and she, you go round to the tacho, and you can see it
> **sending it well past seven to ten thousand revs.**"

**第三处**（`@194884`，更完整）：

> "…the idle jet, sixty-nine sizes smaller… Now, we put the water cell on, and then…
> you'll be able to take it over idling.
> **Then we'll re-set the timing, and bring the timing right around to buggery in advance.
> You'll hear the motor smooth out and everything else, and then she'll go right up
> to ten thousand revs.** Okay, with the jets blocked. **Then we take off the fuel.**"

⇒ ★ **操作序列**：① 封堵喷油嘴（只有怠速油路）→ ② 装上水电池（cell）→ ③ **重设正时、大幅提前**
→ ④ 发动机变平顺 → ⑤ **转速冲到 10,000** → ⑥ **切断燃油**。

### 5.2 「低于 500 转」（Tape 3 @255776）

> "You do need to pull the carburettors up a bit, because you will drop down on revs.
> She'll still run, but **she'll want to drop down well below 500 revs,
> and motors don't like running that low**…
> Initially for doing the transfer over onto the cell, the distributor stays there,
> the motor's still running, **what I do then is I bring the distributor ahead.**
> When I feel the motor and carburettor start to die… **I bring it further forward**…"

⇒ ★ 这条与老师「**最低怠速 60 rpm**」方向一致（都在讲"能跌到极低"），
但**「60 rpm」这个数字未在本全套命中**（最接近的就是这句 "well below 500"）⏳。

### 5.3 反接电池 → 全转速（Tape 1 已见雏形，全套多处）

> "'…the one what says positive I put on negative, the one what says negative I put on positive
> and **started the motor up and she goes full revs. Rev rev rev rev rev smooth as glass**…'"

⇒ 与老师说的「**正负极不能接反**」是同一现象的**原话语境**
（⚠️ 本库安全判据不变：接反 = 筒体蚀刻 + 短路 + 爆炸风险，见 `18 §三`）。

### 5.4 V8 Rover「830–850 磅」与内爆做功的物理描述（`@245345` 附近）

> "…Now **at 830 to 850 pounds in a V8 Rover at the top of the stroke**,
> releasing that poundage on the downstroke…"

⇒ Joe 用"**人的手被推**"的比喻讲真空/阻力（供内爆做功机制讨论引用）。
