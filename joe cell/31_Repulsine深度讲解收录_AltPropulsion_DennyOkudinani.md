## 四、关键引文（逐字 · 含时间码）

1. **核心论点**（[00:01:03]）：
   > "all of the repulsines that we've ever seen today that have been documented in terms of pictures are most likely … never flew. They were probably some sort of prototype … to demonstrate fundamental principles … which could be used for a repulsine which actually did fly."
2. **蓝绿→银光**（[00:48:44]，引 Schauberger 原话）：
   > "the apparatus … at the first attempt it rose upwards trailing a **blue-green and then silver-colored glow**."
3. **机制假说**（[00:48:57–49:49]）：
   > "perhaps there's a sort of **concentrated coronal discharge** … **triboluminescence** takes place when a material is mechanically stressed by rubbing against something else. In this case, the something else is the air. … the repulsine most likely behaved as an **advanced pressure transformer or Van de Graaff generator** … having a concentrated charge of ionization right below the cowl."
4. **孤子定义**（[00:55:50]）：
   > "a soliton is a **self-reinforcing wave packet having a constant velocity** … of permanent form … localized within a region. It can interact with other solitons and it can emerge from the collision unchanged **except with phase shift**."
5. **马赫原理（其形式化）**（[00:54:09–54:48]）：
   > "Mach's principle of impulsive forces: if the universe is a closed box, then the constituent parts are just basically subsystems. Mass, energy, and inertia are transferred through **corridors of interaction**."
6. **扯断锚栓**（[01:00:42]，引 Coats 书）：
   > "the machine generated such a powerful levitation force that when it was first switched on without Schauberger's permission, it **sheared the six 1/4-inch diameter tensile steel anchor bolts** and shot upwards …"
7. **乳化剂更正**（[01:06:21]）：
   > "**There was never an emulsifier needed.** … the only case that you ever hear of an emulsifier being mentioned within Schauberger's work is with **mixtures**."
8. **航业界反证**（[01:06:54]，引 Jörg 2019）：
   > "There is interest from the aircraft industry. It was remodeled some years ago, but they told us **there wasn't any effect**."
9. **自我诚实声明**（[01:09:53]）：
   > "I **did not test it**. This one probably wouldn't be good for testing. It's **weak**. But … with all of the files that I've open sourced, you can go and rebuild this … use a **CNC machine** … and solve the mystery of the **upper chamber**."
10. **Puthoff 线索**（[01:08:54]）：
    > "This was **reproduced by Puthoff Lab** when they did research back in … He's disappeared now."
